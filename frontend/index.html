WHO WE ARE

ASK Education Fund

ASK Bagbin Education Fund

ASK Bagbin Education Fund is a non-profit education foundation established to address persistent educational inequalities in Ghana.

The Foundation responds to systemic barriers that limit access to education, reduce school completion rates and undermine learning outcomes, particularly among girls, children from low-income households and learners in rural and underserved communities.

Guided by national education priorities and aligned with Sustainable Development Goal 4, the Foundation delivers targeted interventions including scholarships and sponsorships, learning support, advocacy and investments in educational infrastructure.

OUR VISION

A Future Where Every Child Has an Opportunity to Learn

Our vision is to create a future where Ghana becomes a model of educational opportunity, in which every child regardless of background or circumstance has access to quality education.
