Research & Evidence

Evidence on completion, gender disparities, foundational learning and wealth-related education outcomes.

Evidence Highlighted in the 2025 Framework

GenderGirls account for approximately 52% of upper-secondary dropouts nationally.
Wealth71% of children from the richest households complete upper secondary, compared with 9% from the poorest households.
Child labour30% of children aged 5–17 are reported as engaged in child labour.
Foundational learningRural and poverty-affected communities record lower Grade 3 literacy and numeracy outcomes.

The figures and sources are presented in the Foundation’s 2025 Policy and Governance Framework.
