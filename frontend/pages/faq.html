Common Questions

What does the Foundation support?

Scholarships and sponsorships, learning support, educational materials, infrastructure, advocacy and community engagement.

Who does the Foundation prioritise?

The policy framework highlights girls, children from low-income households, and learners in rural and underserved communities.

How is the Foundation governed?

A Board/Executive Council provides strategic, fiduciary, risk and mission oversight, supported by standing committees.

Where can I find the official policy document?

Visit the Policy Briefs page to open the 2025 Policy and Governance Framework.
