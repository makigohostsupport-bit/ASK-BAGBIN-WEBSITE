Privacy Notice

Information about how website enquiries and scholarship applications should be handled.

Information we may receive

The website may receive contact details, scholarship application information and documents submitted voluntarily through its forms.

How information should be used

Information should be used only for the purpose for which it was submitted, programme administration, communication and legitimate internal reporting.

Access and retention

Administrative access should be limited to authorised personnel. The Foundation should define and publish an approved retention schedule before collecting live applications.

Before launch

This notice should be reviewed by the Foundation’s legal/administrative advisers and updated with the final privacy contact, retention periods and applicable legal requirements.
