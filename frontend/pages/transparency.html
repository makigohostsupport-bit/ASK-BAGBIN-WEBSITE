ACCOUNTABILITY

Transparency & Accountability

How the Foundation intends to maintain accountability, financial oversight and fair programme implementation.

Accountability commitments

Annual reportsPublic annual reports covering programme activities, achievements and financial performance.
Audited statementsAnnual audited financial statements to support sound financial management and compliance.
External auditsIndependent external audits conducted periodically to verify financial records and strengthen oversight.
Fair selectionClear documentation of beneficiary selection criteria and resource allocation.

Community engagement

The policy framework provides for town hall meetings, stakeholder consultations and outreach activities to promote inclusiveness, feedback and local ownership.

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning

Indicators include scholarship retention and completion, educational progression, attendance and retention, foundational literacy and numeracy gains, gender equity, infrastructure utilisation and safety, and community perception and behavioural change.
