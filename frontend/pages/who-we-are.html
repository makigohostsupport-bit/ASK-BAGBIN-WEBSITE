ABOUT US

Who We Are

Inclusive and equitable education for vulnerable learners across Ghana.

Why the Foundation Exists

Education access has improved in Ghana, but significant disparities persist across regions, income groups and gender. The Foundation’s programming is designed around evidence of gaps in school completion, learning outcomes and participation.

The Foundation works with government institutions, development partners, private-sector organisations and communities to expand opportunity and strengthen learning environments.

Core Objectives

  • Supply free educational materials to less privileged children and institutions.
  • Support education infrastructure in underserved communities.
  • Provide scholarships and sponsorships to needy but brilliant learners.
  • Advance education advocacy.
