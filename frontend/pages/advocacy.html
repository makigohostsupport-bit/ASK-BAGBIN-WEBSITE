Advocacy & Community Engagement

Using evidence, policy engagement and community participation to promote school retention and equity.

Evidence-Based Advocacy

The Foundation grounds its interventions in national education data, peer-reviewed research and official government reports. Its advocacy agenda includes child protection, school retention, gender equity and quality learning outcomes.

Community engagement

The policy framework calls for periodic town hall meetings, stakeholder consultations and outreach activities to promote inclusiveness, feedback and local ownership.

Policy Alignment

  • Ghana Education Strategic Plan
  • Ghana Education Service performance priorities
  • United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4 — Quality Education
  • National equity and gender inclusion frameworks
