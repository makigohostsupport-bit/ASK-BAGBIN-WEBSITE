About the Foundation

A Ghanaian non-profit education foundation working to remove financial, social and structural barriers to education.

Our Foundation

ASK Bagbin Education Fund is a non-profit education foundation established to address persistent educational inequalities in Ghana. The Foundation focuses particularly on girls, children from low-income households, and learners in rural and underserved communities.

Its work combines scholarships and sponsorships, learning support, advocacy for child protection and school retention, and investment in educational infrastructure.

VisionGhana as a model of educational opportunity where every child can access quality education, complete school and contribute meaningfully to society.
MissionAdvance inclusive and equitable education by removing barriers to learning, providing scholarships and resources, and investing in safe learning environments.
Legal statusIncorporated under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992) as a company limited by guarantee.
