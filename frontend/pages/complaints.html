A clear channel for questions, concerns and feedback about the Foundation’s website and programmes.

Send feedback

For a complaint, safeguarding concern, correction request or general feedback, use the Contact form or email info@askbagbinedufund.org.

Before launchThe Foundation should approve a formal complaints-handling procedure, response timelines, escalation route and safeguarding escalation contacts. Those operational details are not specified in the supplied policy document.
