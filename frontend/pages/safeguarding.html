A public commitment to safe, inclusive and respectful engagement with learners and communities.

Our safeguarding standard

The Foundation’s policy identifies child protection and school retention advocacy as part of its work. This website therefore treats learner privacy and safety as a core publishing principle.

  • Do not publish personal learner information without appropriate approval.
  • Use approved images and stories that respect dignity and safety.
  • Provide a clear channel for safeguarding concerns and complaints.
  • Limit access to sensitive application records to authorised administrators.

Important: A detailed operational safeguarding policy should be approved by the Foundation before public launch. This page is a public commitment, not a substitute for that policy.
