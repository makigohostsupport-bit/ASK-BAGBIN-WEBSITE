Scholarship Programme

The Foundation’s policy framework identifies scholarships and sponsorships as a targeted intervention for vulnerable learners. Programme details should be published through clear eligibility, documentation, selection and monitoring procedures.

Programme principles

  • Equity and fairness in beneficiary selection.
  • Transparent documentation and resource allocation.
  • Monitoring of retention, completion and educational progression.
  • Support for vulnerable learners facing financial barriers.
