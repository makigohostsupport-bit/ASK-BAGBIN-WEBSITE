The Foundation’s scholarship and sponsorship focus, based on its 2025 policy framework.

Scholarship Eligibility

The policy framework states that the Foundation offers scholarships and sponsorships to needy but brilliant learners. It also identifies girls, children from low-income households and learners in rural and underserved communities as priority groups for its broader work.

Publication statusDetailed eligibility rules, application windows and selection criteria should be published only after approval by the Foundation.
